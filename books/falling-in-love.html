Falling in Love with the Hope of a Promise

Love story · October 14, 2026

She is waiting for him to say what they are. He is waiting for a test result that will tell him whether he is allowed to love her at all.

Neither knows the other is waiting. Neither knows the other is keeping a secret to protect them. And neither knows that their story began long before they met, on a gray lake, with a red bandana and a borrowed fishing rod.

Anna and Raymond lived the same two years: the same rainy Saturdays, the same unanswered messages, a summer wedding, an umbrella too small for two, and a key that was never given. Told backward, from the night a text finally arrives to the day it all began, their story is narrated by the two promises that were never quite spoken, hers and his. Read her side or his first, then let the truth tell you what really happened.

A slow-burn love story about what we hear in the silence, what we hide to protect each other, and the courage it takes to finally say it out loud.

Content note: includes references to sexual assault (not depicted on the page), a parent's terminal hereditary illness, and childhood abandonment.
