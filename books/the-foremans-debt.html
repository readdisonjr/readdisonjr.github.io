The Foreman's Debt

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 6

eBook $9.99

Twenty-one birthday cards. Every one returned unopened.

Gus Reyes has been the Hale Six's protector, cook and grumbling second father since the night their parents vanished. On Christmas night he finally sees the face he has been afraid of for twenty-one years: the young man the Swiss police want for murder is his son, Mateo, taken to Valencia as a little boy and raised to believe his father sold his mother out.

In 2007 Gus signed one line in a dead man's Book of Debts to save that boy's life: one service, unnamed, at the holder's call. Now the line belongs to Don Esteban Cardona, the gentle old godfather of Halcyon's Iberian branch, and he has named his price. He wants Jonah, the boy who remembers. From the ice of Pittsburgh's bridges to the rice fields of the Albufera, the tunnels of Gibraltar and the bonfires of Las Fallas, the Hale Six will fight to pay a debt without paying it with one of their own.

All six. Or nothing.
