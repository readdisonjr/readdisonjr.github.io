Gotta Keep Moving

Novel

Ask Cal Reed how he's doing, and he'll give you the same answer every time: "I'm good."

He has a roof over his head, a steady job, a wife who stayed, two healthy kids, and a pew at church on Sunday morning. By every measure he was raised to respect, he has nothing to complain about.

So every night at 2:14 a.m., when he's sitting on the edge of the bed staring at the floor with a heaviness that sleep won't fix, he keeps it to himself. Nobody wants to hear a grown man say he's tired in his bones. Nobody except Bishop, the seventy-pound gray-muzzled rescue dog waiting quietly outside the bedroom door.

Gotta Keep Moving: Even When the World Weighs You Down is an honest, deeply human novel about faith, marriage, fatherhood, and the quiet weight carried by the men who smile so nobody will ask—and what happens when a man finally learns he doesn't have to carry it alone.
