Copycat

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 5

eBook $9.99

Alone, undercover, eleven years old. Her voice is her only weapon.

Lou Hale never applied to the Institut Montclair, an elite girls' school in a château above Lake Geneva. But the scholarship letter is signed in her mother's handwriting, and Lou says yes before anyone can stop her. On her first day the school takes her watch, and with it her only line to her five siblings.

Montclair has a choir, a clock tower, and a cellar no one is allowed to enter. For forty years it has quietly collected the secrets of the world's most powerful families through their daughters, and the iron door that guards them opens only to two voices speaking at once. Lou can copy anyone. Now she has to decide whether her mother sent her there to save her, or to use her.

All six. Or nothing.
