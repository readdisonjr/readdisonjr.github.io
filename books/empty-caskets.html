Empty Caskets

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 1

Six siblings. A vanished parents' conspiracy. No way back.

When their CIA-officer parents vanish on a mission and the agency buries two empty caskets, six siblings aged 10 to 23 bargain their way into the CIA to find the truth — and learn their father spent their whole childhood training them for this day.

To find their parents, they must finish their training.

Empty Caskets is Book One of The Hale Six, a family espionage thriller from R. E. Addison Jr. about loyalty, secrets, and the people we'd lie for.
