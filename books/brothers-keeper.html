Brother's Keeper

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 1 · October 9, 2026

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

At 1:47 a.m., four of the five Russell brothers are awake and arguing in the group chat. The fifth, Ronelle, a Cook County public defender who was just held in contempt in open court, has gone silent.

Then Judge Eleanor Whitlock is found shot dead in the passenger seat of a car, and Ronelle wakes up behind the wheel with the murder weapon in his hand. The warrant was signed before anyone knew she was dead. The gun belonged to their father, a Chicago police detective buried twelve years ago. And the prosecutor's office that wants Ronelle convicted employs his own brother.

Ron, the soldier. Rashaad, the nurse. Ryan, the hacker. Rayvon, the prosecutor. To save the youngest, the Russell brothers will have to break every rule in Chicago and face the one secret their mother has kept for twenty-eight years.

Someone has been watching their family for a very long time. Someone who calls on Sunday nights. Someone no one has ever seen. They call her the Gardener.

A Chicago crime thriller about family, loyalty, and how far brothers will go for each other.
