The Resonance

Supernatural thriller

ONE FOR EACH OF THEM.

Ronelle Hendricks is the youngest of seven, which means he is always the last to know anything.

So he is the last one out the door the night the sky splits open over Grandma Ruth's farm, and seven streaks of light fall into the field. One for each of them.

By morning, Ryan can hold fire. Ron can lift the creek. Rashaad hears the ground, Rayvon can't stay on it, Rhonda crackles like a storm, and Ronnesha can hear every thought in the house. Only Ronelle's stone is cracked into six pieces—and it only wakes up when his brothers and sisters are near.

Then the faceless things in the tree line start watching the farmhouse. And Grandma Ruth is hiding a secret of her own: the sky has split open over this family before, and whatever wants those stones is coming to collect them.

A high-stakes supernatural thriller about seven siblings, one unbreakable bond, and the power that only works when a family stands together.
