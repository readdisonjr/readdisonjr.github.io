Black Coffee

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 4

eBook $9.99

Every secret in the Agency starts with one cup.

The night before his twenty-first birthday, Eli Hale is bolting steel forty stories above Columbus when Vivian Marsh climbs up to meet him. A prisoner is dead in his cell, her coffee cup on the table, and every agency in America is hunting her. She hands Eli a sealed envelope: if she's dead by Christmas, open it, and burn the place down.

From a break-in at CIA Headquarters to Christmas in Vienna, a salt mine under the Alps and a hostage trade on a frozen lake, the six follow a twenty-six-year-old photograph of their parents on a snowy train platform. Someone arranged that meeting. Someone held the camera. And the man who knows why is about to become Director of the CIA.

All six. Or nothing.
