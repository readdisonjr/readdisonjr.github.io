What Raymond Saw

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 8

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

Raymond Russell taught five boys to count off at his table every Sunday of their lives. He was a Chicago policeman for twenty-eight years. He has never once refused his family an answer, until now.

Where were you on the night of December 18, 1997?

Ronelle has asked his father seven times. Seven times Raymond has turned his hat in his hands and walked away. Then a woman meets Ronelle at the lake on the first warm night of the year and gives him an address on Rush Street, a bar with a piano in the window, and a bartender who has been waiting a long time to meet him.

The bartender remembers Ray Russell. And some things in the house on Calumet Avenue have been kept even longer.

From a Gold Coast bar to the lakefront rocks to the river mouths of the South Side, What Raymond Saw is Book Eight in the Brother's Keeper saga: a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score and never leave one of their own behind. Read the series in order.

Five brothers. One father. One question he won't answer.
