Blood Trust

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 5

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

A month before his wedding, prosecutor Rayvon Russell walks into the courthouse at 26th and California to stand beside a frightened witness. By four o'clock the building is locked down, and Rayvon is on his knees in a stairwell with a deputy's gun at his head.

The man holding it doesn't want money. He wants a woman.

Outside, his brothers count the minutes the way their father taught them. Their mother is under a federal subpoena she refuses to explain. The house on Calumet Avenue is not as safe as they thought. And the woman the deputy is asking for is the one person the Russells can't afford to hand over, and can't afford to keep.

From a Chicago courthouse to the river country below Natchez to a murder trial in Washington, Blood Trust is Book Five in the Brother's Keeper saga: a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score and never leave one of their own behind. Read the series in order.

Five brothers. One wedding. A debt that comes due in blood.
