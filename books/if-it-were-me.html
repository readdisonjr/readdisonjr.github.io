If It Were Me

Small-town thriller

Some letters were never meant to be found.

In the corner behind the furnace, young Theo Kessler finds something he was never supposed to see: handwritten pages about crimes no one in Calder Falls, Pennsylvania, ever solved.

Across town, in a basement under the police station, the Cold Case Unit is two steel desks and a boiler. Detectives Danny Cho and Ruth have been handed the cases nobody upstairs wants, until a faded receipt and a trail of handwritten letters start pointing somewhere no one wants to look.

The deeper they dig, the closer the answers come to home.

From R. E. Addison Jr., author of Three Lines, a gripping small-town thriller about grief, guilt, and the question that haunts every family with a secret: what would you do, if it were you?
