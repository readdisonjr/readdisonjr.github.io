The Infamous Fame

Crime thriller

They got exactly what they wanted.

On the last Friday before Christmas, two teenagers park at the gravel turnout on Lake Corbin Road, the darkest stretch of road in Bellport, where everyone at Hogan High goes to be alone. By morning, the town has a story it will never stop telling.

Nora Kessinger has spent her whole career at the Bellport Ledger, and now the paper is dying around her. Then the letters start arriving, signed by someone who calls himself the Infamous Fame, and every one of them is meant to be printed.

The killer wants an audience. The paper needs readers. And the closer Nora gets to the truth, the harder it becomes to tell who is chasing the story and who is feeding it.

A gripping crime thriller about headlines, obsession, and the terrible price of being remembered, from R. E. Addison Jr., author of Three Lines.
