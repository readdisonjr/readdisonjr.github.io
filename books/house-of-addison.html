House of Addison: The Sworn Flame

YA dragon fantasy

When the oath grows old, the fire will remember.

For three hundred years, the dragons of House Addison have kept their oath. Tonight, they burn the Keep to the ground.

On the night of the Festival of Flames, the family's own dragons turn on the home they have guarded for centuries. Lord Ron Addison falls holding back his great black warbeast, and sixteen-year-old Kamiron is left to lead a broken house with nothing but a sword, a temper, and a traitor to hunt.

But ten-year-old Syrai, the youngest dragon trainer the family has ever known, hears what no one else can: a song drifting down from the Ashen Mountains. The poison everyone blames proves harmless. The traitor may be innocent. And their mother is hiding a secret burned into her hands.

With an army at the gates and an enemy inside the walls, a brother and sister must uncover the truth about their family's oath before it costs them everything.
