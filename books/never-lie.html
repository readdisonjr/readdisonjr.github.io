Never Lie

Thriller · Three Lines, Book 4

The first line was the hardest one to keep.

For thirty-one years, Tess Malloy has kept the promise she made in a treehouse on Fig Street: never lie to each other.

Then, at 9:30 one August night, the minute her family blinks a flashlight at the window, a photograph arrives from a number in Mexico. A ten-year-old boy at a window, holding an old red flashlight against the glass. Under it, three words: Promise three. Keep it.

Tess knows those eyes. She knows whose promise it is. And for the first time in her life, she tells no one.

Someone else is looking for the boy. Someone with money from nowhere and men who go where they're sent. Every question Tess asks lights a path to him. Every secret she keeps breaks the one line her friends' children have built their lives on.

Everyone who loves that boy has a reason to stay silent. The children want the truth anyway.

Never Lie is Book Four and the finale of the Three Lines series by R. E. Addison Jr.

Thirty-one years ago, five kids swore it. This time, somebody has to keep it.
