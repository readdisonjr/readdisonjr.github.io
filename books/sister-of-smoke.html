Sister of Smoke

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 9

eBook $9.99

A security camera in Bern caught a woman walking out of the Federal Palace with Nadia Hale's face. At that exact moment, Nadia was kneeling in the snow beside her brother in Zurich.

It wasn't her. Everyone in the Hale family knows that. But "it's not you" isn't the same thing as knowing who it was, and Jonah, who never forgets anything he has seen, can't stop going over the photograph. Back home, a patient at Nadia's clinic swears she saw Nadia on High Street on a day Nadia was somewhere else. Someone has been wearing her life like a coat.

Now the six Hale siblings have to find a woman who doesn't want to be found, before the person using Nadia's face finishes whatever she started, and before the family learns what it costs to chase smoke.

All six. Or nothing.

Sister of Smoke is Book Nine of The Hale Six, a family thriller series by R. E. Addison Jr.
