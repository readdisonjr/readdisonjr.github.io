Never Leave

Thriller · Three Lines, Book 3

Thirty years ago, five kids carved three promises into a treehouse wall. Now the last one is coming due.

On the empty lot on Fig Street where the treehouse burned in 1998, a stranger walks up to the old pepper tree in the middle of the night and leaves a red flashlight in the dirt—a message meant only for the people who were there the night of the fire.

Days later, someone starts photographing their children.

As a new power rises along the old border routes and a thirty-year-old ghost steps out of the dark, the survivors of Fig Street have to face what really happened the night their childhood ended—and keep the one promise their past already broke.

Never leave each other.

Book Three of the Three Lines series, from R. E. Addison Jr.
