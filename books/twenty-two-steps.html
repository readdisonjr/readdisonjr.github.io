Twenty-Two Steps

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 9

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

Three words, whispered in a hospital room before dawn. Ronelle Russell hasn't told anyone what they were. He doesn't have to. By the first week of January, somebody has broken into the family's car, somebody is watching a little house on St. Catherine Street in Natchez, and somebody in Washington already knows.

Some debts were written down long before Hattie Henderson ever held a pen.

Under a kitchen floor in Mississippi lies a book that names a debt on every powerful family in Chicago and Memphis. Whoever holds it holds the city. The Easter women want it. Washington wants it buried. And a woman in a federal cell wants it more than any of them.

Five brothers, one sister and their mother have one thing left to decide: what this family is for.

Twenty-Two Steps is the final book of the Brother's Keeper saga, a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score and never leave one of their own behind. Read the series in order.

Five brothers. Twenty-two steps. One last Sunday.
