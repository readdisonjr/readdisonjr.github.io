The Sixth Chair

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 6

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

The day before Thanksgiving, a lawyer knocks on the back door of 4510 Calumet Avenue, the door family never knocks on, and hands Denise Russell eleven pages. The house her husband bought for her forty-two years ago is not theirs. It has been sold.

The sale closes the Monday after Easter.

Denise won't say how it happened. Raymond won't move aside to let one of his own sons in. And at the long table where the Russell brothers have always counted off, one through five, there is a sixth chair, and somebody wants it.

Somebody has been watching the house. Somebody has been taking pictures. And every Sunday at four o'clock, the kitchen phone rings again.

From a South Side kitchen through a Chicago winter to Easter morning, The Sixth Chair is Book Six in the Brother's Keeper saga: a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score and never leave one of their own behind. Read the series in order.

Five brothers. One table. Until Easter.
