Six Keys

Family espionage thriller · The Hale Six, Book 3

eBook $9.99

Every spy on Earth wants what's in his pocket.

After Tbilisi, word leaks to every intelligence service in the world: the only complete copy of the Ledger, the secret names of forty people who risked everything for the CIA, belongs to the Hale kids. To protect it, Marcus splits the key into six pieces and hides one in each sibling. Now the hunters don't need the drive. They need the children.

From an erupting volcano in Iceland to the ghost stations of Berlin, a flooded vault in Singapore, a hacker convention in Las Vegas and a torch-lit auction on the cliffs of Cornwall, Marcus must outthink a charming British spymaster, a Russian colonel with a secret, and a shadow inside the CIA, while trusting the one friend he has never met in person.

All six. Or nothing.
