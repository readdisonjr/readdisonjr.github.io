Caged Voice

Psychological novel · December 18, 2025

Some houses are built to shield us. Others trap us.

To twenty thousand fans in a London arena, Noah Fields is a star. To himself, he is a man holding his breath. Because Noah carries a house that exists only in his mind: a dark mansion lined with locked doors, whispering memories, and a basement he has spent his entire life avoiding. Every room holds a version of his past. Every hallway echoes with the voice he has tried to silence.

As his music career soars, so does the pressure to carry the pain of everyone who hears his songs. Fans call him strong. Critics call him broken. Behind the spotlight, Noah is unraveling, torn between the man the world believes him to be and the one he is afraid to face. Therapy pushes him to open doors he has kept sealed for years, while fame blurs the line between healing and self-destruction. And with every step forward, the voice inside his head grows louder.

Caged Voice is a raw psychological novel about trauma, identity, and the long road toward forgiveness. It is not a story about being fearless. It is a story about choosing hope.
