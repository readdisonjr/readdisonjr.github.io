House of Addison: The Truest Blood

YA dragon fantasy

THE OATH IS ENDING. THE FIRE REMEMBERS.

Three hundred years ago, a promise bound the dragons of Aurelan to the House of Addison. Now that promise is running out, and every dragon in the kingdom can feel it.

Ron Addison has been dragged from a drowning tower. His son Kamiron nearly died to free him. And ten-year-old Syrai, the girl the dragons trust above anyone, has been carried north on the back of a vast black dragon by the Heir of a line her family wronged.

Across forty provinces, from the Glass Coast to the Southern Isles, dragons are turning white-eyed and wild. In the capital of Highcrown, King Halvard's forges are making iron collars by the hundred. And on the Long Night, when the sun goes down at noon, the oath must be answered at the Cradle by the one whose blood is truest.

Latisha Addison has spent years believing she knows what that answer will cost.

She may have been wrong about everything.

The epic conclusion to the HOUSE OF ADDISON trilogy.
