The Right Weight Part One

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 3, Part One

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

For twelve years, Detective Raymond Russell's grave held a box of sandbags. Then the cemetery dug it up, and four strong men couldn't lift it.

Public defender Ronelle Russell thought the war on Calumet Avenue was finally over. Then someone cuts the lock on the cemetery vault at midnight. Someone has left a single gardenia on his father's grave every Easter for twelve years. The girl who drew backwards moons on the sidewalk has just moved in next door. And in Washington, one of the most powerful lawyers in America wants that coffin sealed, tonight.

The only woman who knows what's really inside will tell Ronelle just one thing: Ask Earl where his daughter went.

The Right Weight: Part One is the first half of Book Three in the Brother's Keeper saga: a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score and never leave one of their own behind.

Five brothers. One grave. And a weight that was never sand.
