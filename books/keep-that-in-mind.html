Keep That in Mind

Daily motivation · 365 messages

365 days. 365 messages. One push forward at a time.

Keep That in Mind is a daily book of real talk for anyone building something: a better life, a stronger faith, a steadier mind. Every page gives you one bold message and a short explanation to carry with you until tomorrow.

Twelve months. Twelve themes. New beginnings, self-worth, discipline, faith, peace, haters, money, battles, mindset, your circle, gratitude and finishing strong.

"God removed people from your life because He heard what you didn't." "Broke is a stage, not a sentence." "Certain battles keep coming because you keep coming out stronger."

Start on Day 1 or start today. Just keep that in mind.
