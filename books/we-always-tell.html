We Always Tell

Thriller · Three Lines, Book 2

Some promises don't stay buried.

The friends who grew up on Fig Street thought the hardest part of their past was finally behind them. They survived the fallout of the secrets they kept since they were twelve. They paid the price.

They were wrong.

What happened the summer the treehouse burned was bigger than any one traitor, and the reckoning isn't over. Now old boxes are being opened, a new list of names is surfacing inside the intelligence community, and the survivors of Fig Street are being pulled back to the night of the fire—and to the one question they swore they would never ask.

The gripping sequel to Three Lines: Whatever Happens, We Never Tell. A thriller about loyalty, buried truths, and what it costs when silence is no longer an option.
