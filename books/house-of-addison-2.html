House of Addison: The Burning Oath

YA dragon fantasy

The dragons have asked their question. Now Syrai has to answer it.

At dawn in the Cradle, hundreds of dragons sit in silence and wait for a ten-year-old girl to decide their fate. Will House Addison chain them again, or set them free? Syrai has promised she will never use a chain. But she can't break an oath she doesn't understand, and if she gets it wrong, she could destroy the dragons, her family, and everyone who depends on them.

While Kamiron fights to hold a broken house together, Syrai and her dragon Nyxara go hunting for the truth buried under three hundred years of stories: what the oath really is, who forged it, and what it will cost to end it.

Some promises were made to be kept. This one was made to burn.

House of Addison, Book Two.
