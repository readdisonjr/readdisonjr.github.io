The Cost of Perfection

Novel

"What is sold is gone. You will not miss it. That is the point."

In a narrow alley in Seoul, there is a pawnshop that only people in pain can find. Its gentle keeper pours you a cup of barley tea and makes a quiet offer: lay your hand on his brass scale, and he will take the part of you that you are most ashamed of. A stutter. A temper. A laugh that won't stop. A memory you can't survive.

Seventeen-year-old Ha-eun sells the stutter that has made her a target since childhood. Her father wrestles with the rage he inherited. An aspiring actress sells what holds her back in every audition, while another woman trades away a night seven years ago that she has never been able to forgive herself for.

At first, their new lives feel flawless.

But nothing in that shop is truly free—and perfection costs more than any of them were told.

Told across one year in Seoul, from the January snow to the longest night of winter, The Cost of Perfection is a heartbreaking novel about family, sacrifice, and the imperfections that make us who we are.
