The Gardener's Table

Chicago crime thriller · Brother's Keeper, Book 2

Part of the Brother's Keeper series. See all the books in reading order.

Every family has a secret recipe. The Russells are about to find out what's in theirs.

Public defender Ronelle Russell thought he had finally seen the worst of what Chicago's South Side could do to his family. He was wrong. Someone has spent sixty years quietly tending a garden of bought judges, buried cops and convenient deaths, and that someone has been far closer to the Russell family's Sunday table than any of them ever imagined.

The night before federal agents are set to move, the trail goes cold. Then the harvest begins.

One by one, the Russell brothers lose everything. Rashaad's nursing license. Rayvon's career. Ron's freedom. Ryan's last chance. Their father's name. A hacker who signs her work with a backwards moon is living inside their walls, and a letter in their father's own handwriting confesses to a murder he swears he never committed.

To save his family, Ronelle has to follow the garden back to its root: a kitchen in Memphis in 1962, a recipe card hidden for sixty-four years, and four hundred Mason jars in a cellar no one was ever meant to open. And when the case finally reaches a federal courtroom, he will have to put the people he loves most on the witness stand.

The Gardener's Table is the explosive second book in the Brother's Keeper saga: a Black family crime thriller about five brothers who fight, joke, keep score, and never once leave one of their own behind.

Five brothers. One table. And a garden that has been growing longer than any of them have been alive.
