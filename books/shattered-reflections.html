Shattered Reflections

Revenge thriller · October 8, 2026

She inherited a cracked mirror, a dead woman's secrets, and a seat at the table of the family that destroyed hers.

Three weeks after her grandmother's sudden death, Maya Thompson is still pouring coffee at Brewed Awakening and still telling herself that heart failure is just something that happens at seventy-four. Then a stranger in a camel coat slides a card across the counter: Your grandmother owed us. Now you do.

The card bears a single letter: V. In Harlow Bay, that means the Vance family, who own the harbor, the hotels, the newspaper, and the police chief. For thirty years, Maya's grandmother ran their great gray house on the cliff. She never once mentioned it.

Now Evelyn is gone, and she has left Maya three things: an antique mirror kept under a sheet for twenty-four years, a journal written in a code of flowers, and a warning. Trust no one in that house who is kind to you without a reason.

Then the will is read, and a dying billionaire Maya has never met hands her twenty percent of his empire. The Vances want it back. Some of them want her dead.

To learn why, Maya walks through the front door of Calder House and into a family war twenty-four years in the making. What really happened the night her mother died on the Harlow Bridge? And what is hidden behind the glass of the mirror that everyone in town seems willing to kill for?

Maya came looking for answers. She is staying for revenge.

A twisty revenge thriller of buried crimes, stolen birthrights, dangerous love, and an old-money family built on lies. Perfect for readers who love family dynasty drama, domestic suspense, morally gray characters, and slow-burn romance.
