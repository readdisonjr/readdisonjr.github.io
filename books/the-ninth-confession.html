The Ninth Confession

Psychological horror thriller

Nine cassette tapes. Eight names of people he remembers killing. The ninth is his own.

Forensic psychologist Dr. Elias Voss has spent thirty-seven years managing memories that cannot be real: eight murders he remembers committing as a boy. Then a box appears on his doorstep, labeled in his own handwriting. On the first tape, his nine-year-old voice confesses to killing a woman who has not yet been born. Then a little girl calls him Daddy.

His daughter drowned twenty years ago. At least, that is what he remembers.

When a body turns up staged exactly like a crime on the second tape, and a detective searching for her missing brother starts asking how Elias knows details the police never released, the trail leads back to Saint Oran's, a derelict psychiatric hospital on a flooded headland, and to the experiment that turned one frightened child into a vessel for other people's guilt.

Seventeen days. Nine confessions. And a truth that someone, or something, is desperate for him to remember.

The dead don't want justice. They want someone else to remember dying.
