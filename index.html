Three Lines: Whatever Happens, We Never Tell

Thriller · Three Lines, Book 1

Five kids. Three promises carved into a treehouse wall. A fire that same night.

Never lie. Never leave. Whatever happens, never tell.

The summer they were twelve, five friends swore those words with a pocketknife. Hours later the treehouse burned, and Catalina vanished without a trace.

Twenty-eight years later, four of those friends hunt cartels for the CIA. Catalina runs one.

Then a raid in the Sierra Madre ends in a massacre. The Agency is sure someone fed her the plan. And she only trusts people she grew up with.

Now the promise that held them together for twenty-eight years is the one thing that can get them killed.

For fans of Don Winslow's cartel thrillers and childhood-friendship suspense. Some promises cost more than others. Start the Three Lines series today.

Revenge thriller · October 8, 2026

Shattered Reflections

She inherited a cracked mirror, a dead woman's secrets, and a seat at the table of the family that destroyed hers.

Three weeks after her grandmother's sudden death, Maya Thompson is still pouring coffee at Brewed Awakening and still telling herself that heart failure is just something that happens at seventy-four. Then a stranger in a camel coat slides a card across the counter: Your grandmother owed us. Now you do.

Read more

The card bears a single letter: V. In Harlow Bay, that means the Vance family, who own the harbor, the hotels, the newspaper, and the police chief. For thirty years, Maya's grandmother ran their great gray house on the cliff. She never once mentioned it.

Now Evelyn is gone, and she has left Maya three things: an antique mirror kept under a sheet for twenty-four years, a journal written in a code of flowers, and a warning. Trust no one in that house who is kind to you without a reason.

Then the will is read, and a dying billionaire Maya has never met hands her twenty percent of his empire. The Vances want it back. Some of them want her dead.

To learn why, Maya walks through the front door of Calder House and into a family war twenty-four years in the making. What really happened the night her mother died on the Harlow Bridge? And what is hidden behind the glass of the mirror that everyone in town seems willing to kill for?

Maya came looking for answers. She is staying for revenge.

A twisty revenge thriller of buried crimes, stolen birthrights, dangerous love, and an old-money family built on lies. Perfect for readers who love family dynasty drama, domestic suspense, morally gray characters, and slow-burn romance.

Novel

This Pillow Has Heard It All

Everybody leans on Maya. Nobody asks who's holding her.

Maya Brooks is twenty-three, and she is everybody's rock.

Read more

She answers the 2 a.m. calls. She drives across Chicago on eleven dollars of gas to bring a drunk friend home. She sends money to Atlanta, remembers every birthday, and says "I'm fine" eight hours a day to strangers from cubicle 41 of a medical billing call center.

Nobody asks her back.

The only place Maya is allowed to fall apart is her pillow: an old feather pillow in a pale yellow case her grandmother stitched, years ago, with two words in blue thread. Rest, baby.

Over one year of nights, Maya tells that pillow everything she can't say out loud. The overdraft fees. The best friend who calls at 2:14 a.m. and never asks how she is. The mother who only calls when she needs something and still calls her "my strong one." The quiet cellist across the hall who might be the first person to really see her. And Nana, slipping away in Room 114, the one person who ever told Maya she was allowed to put it down.

Told in raw, intimate prose and in poems written in the dark, This Pillow Has Heard It All is a heartbreaking novel about the people who hold the world up all day and come home to a quiet room with nobody to hold them.

For readers who have ever been the strong friend, and for everyone who has cried into a pillow so no one would hear.

About the Author

R. E. Addison Jr. writes thrillers about loyalty, secrets, and the people we'd lie for. Before the stories, there was service. As an Army veteran, he served as a Military Police officer, a Chinook Helicopter Maintainer, and a Petroleum Specialist.

He went on to work as an Airplane, Helicopter, and Rocket Mechanic, a career built on precision, pressure, and knowing that one missed detail can bring everything down. He brings that same discipline to his novels and screenplays.

Influenced by Anthony Horowitz and Jeneva Rose, he lives on a ranch in Montana and is at work on his next novel. His books are available on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited.
