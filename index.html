Three Lines: Whatever Happens, We Never Tell

Thriller · Three Lines, Book 1

Five kids. Three promises carved into a treehouse wall. A fire that same night.

Never lie. Never leave. Whatever happens, never tell.

The summer they were twelve, five friends swore those words with a pocketknife. Hours later the treehouse burned, and Catalina vanished without a trace.

Twenty-eight years later, four of those friends hunt cartels for the CIA. Catalina runs one.

Then a raid in the Sierra Madre ends in a massacre. The Agency is sure someone fed her the plan. And she only trusts people she grew up with.

Now the promise that held them together for twenty-eight years is the one thing that can get them killed.

For fans of Don Winslow's cartel thrillers and childhood-friendship suspense. Some promises cost more than others. Start the Three Lines series today.
